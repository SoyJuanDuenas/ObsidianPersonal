
A **regression problem** is a type of [[supervised statistical learning]] task where the goal is to predict a **continuous numerical value** from one or more input variables.  
  
Given a dataset with input variables $X$ and a target variable $y$, regression aims to learn a function:  
  
$$  
f(X) \approx y  
$$  
where $y$ can take continuous values, such as:  
  
- house price  
- temperature  
- monthly sales  
- waiting time  
- estimated salary  
  
## Examples  
  
- Predicting the **price of a house** based on square footage, location, and number of rooms.  
- Estimating **energy demand** for the next day.  
- Predicting a student’s **final grade** from study hours and attendance.  
- Estimating a person’s **age** from biometric measurements.

[[Root Mean Square Error (RMSE)]] 
[[Mean Absolute Error (MAE)]].
